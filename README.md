# Surrey City Centre campaign map

A static dashboard for the Surrey City Centre provincial riding: supporters found against the votes needed to win, polls shaded by the 2024 result, supporters found, voters not contacted or 2024 supporters who didn't vote, and every contacted household on a labelled street map with one simple filter.

**The page is password-protected.** The team's campaign data (Poll Summary, Interactions and the 2024 supporter voted / did-not-vote lists, with voter names, phone numbers, voter IDs, notes and canvassers removed) is in `data/campaign.enc.js`, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from the team password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations). Without the password the file can't be read, so copying the site or this repository yields nothing usable. Data added since is in `data/campaign.extra.enc.js`, locked with the same key: the phone-bank (T2) households, the door-knocked houses, and the voters file of our supporters on the voters list (voter number, name and address; no phone numbers), which the maps use to read participation reports and to look up whether someone has voted. Everything else is public: `data/public.js` is built from BC open data: Elections BC 2024 boundaries, voting areas, registered voter counts, results by voting place and the poll-to-voting-place assignment; the BC Digital Road Atlas; and BC Address Geocoder address points.

Open the page and enter the team password. The data is decrypted in your browser only; the page's Content-Security-Policy blocks every outbound connection, so nothing is sent anywhere. Closing the tab locks it again. You can also load newer exports (.xlsx) from your own device; they stay in that tab.

**Who has voted.** Load the Elections BC voting participation report (`VotingParticipationReport….csv`) on either map; nothing needs publishing. Each report adds to the ones already loaded, so load each new one as it comes. The list follows the tab between the two maps and is dropped when you lock or close it. Both maps then show how many have voted in each poll and where, and answer "has someone voted?" by name, voter number or address. Our supporters are matched to the report by voter number. Routes, focus scores and T1 households leave out our people the report shows have voted; where a unit has several of our people and only some are in the report, the report can't say which ones, so only that many are taken off.

Libraries: [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com) 1.9.4 and [SheetJS](https://sheetjs.com) 0.20.3, vendored in `vendor/`.
